# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primarily students and their parents buying school supplies. Secondarily, neighborhood locals in Córdoba seeking quick photocopies, small gifts, and stationery. Most visitors arrive from Instagram on mobile — the page is a social-media storefront, not a desktop experience. They want to know: "What do they sell? Where are they? How do I contact them?" — answered in under 10 seconds.

## Product Purpose

A landing page for Pinceladas y algo más, a local stationery and bookstore in Av. Bernardo O'Higgins 5671, Córdoba, Argentina. Its job is to convert Instagram followers into physical customers: drive foot traffic, earn WhatsApp messages, and grow the social following. Success means a neighbor that finds the page goes to the store.

## Brand Personality

Colorida, cálida, local. The voice is warm and direct — like a neighbor who knows your name. Expressive and creative without being loud for its own sake. Feels like it was made by the same hands that painted the logo, not by a marketing team.

## Anti-references

- Big stationery chains (Office Depot, Staples): corporate, cold, no soul
- Generic template landing pages: could belong to any store, anywhere — no identity
- Infantile school aesthetics: flat primary-color crayons-and-rulers look

## Design Principles

1. **El barrio se nota** — Every choice signals "local, human store." No franchise energy, no cold professionalism.
2. **Color como lenguaje** — The paint-stroke logo is the brand's visual identity. Design amplifies that energy; it does not tone it down.
3. **Calidez antes que impresionar** — Warmth and approachability over visual complexity for its own sake.
4. **Legibilidad primero, arte después** — Decorative elements serve the message; they never compete with it.
5. **Pensado para el celular** — Most traffic arrives from Instagram. Every layout decision defaults to mobile-first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow WCAG AA as a baseline: sufficient color contrast on all text, keyboard-navigable CTAs, meaningful alt text on images. No formal compliance requirement, but good contrast and legible type are non-negotiable given the mixed age range of the audience.
